# Standard Operating Procedure: Weekly exception review

This SOP is a record of observed work, not a guarantee that unobserved cases are covered. Review low-confidence and unknown material before adopting it as a standard.

## Purpose and scope

- **Purpose:** Prepare the reviewed report for the weekly operations meeting
- **Scope:** Operations reviews a dated CSV before the weekly meeting
- **Evidence confidence:** HIGH (92%) — Repeated in the observed session

## Required resources

- **Source CSV** (required) — A dated export supplied by the operations team
  - **Evidence:** HIGH (92%) — Repeated in the observed session
- **Optional notes** (optional) — No description was captured.
  - **Evidence:** MODERATE (68%) — Observed once

## Observed tools

| Tool | Observed role | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Spreadsheet editor | Inspect and annotate the source | HIGH (92%) — Repeated in the observed session |

## Procedure

### 1. Open the source

- **Action:** Open the dated CSV in the spreadsheet editor
- **Purpose:** Establish the review period before filtering
- **Tools:** Spreadsheet editor
- **Inputs:** Source CSV
- **Expected result:** Open review sheet
- **Evidence:** HIGH (92%) — Repeated in the observed session

### 2. Review exceptions

- **Action:** Filter rows marked for follow-up and add notes
- **Purpose:** Separate items that need an owner decision
- **Tools:** Spreadsheet editor
- **Inputs:** Open review sheet
- **Expected result:** Reviewed report
- **Evidence:** LOW (32%) — Partial evidence only

## Decision table

| Condition observed | Observed path | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Does the row have a follow-up marker? | Add it to the reviewed report | MODERATE (68%) — Observed once |
| If No marker | Leave the row unchanged | alternative recorded |

## Exceptions and variations

- **Missing source date** applies when The export has no date in its filename. Difference: Ask the operations owner to confirm the review period before continuing
  - **Evidence:** LOW (32%) — Partial evidence only

## Expected outputs

- **Reviewed report** (required) — A report ready for the weekly review
  - **Evidence:** HIGH (92%) — Repeated in the observed session
