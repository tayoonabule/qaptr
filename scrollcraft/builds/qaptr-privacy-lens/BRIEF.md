## Step 1 defaults from the existing Qaptr site

- **What this is and who it is for:** Qaptr is a private-beta macOS product for people who need to remember, automate, review, and communicate the work they do across a day.
- **What the visitor should believe:** Qaptr can turn the invisible shape of a work session into useful context without interrupting the work or giving up control.
- **Next action:** Join the waitlist.
- **Available assets:** Existing Qaptr token system, wordmark, inline workflow diagrams, and `qaptr-workspace.webp`. No external image generation required for the first pass.
- **Art direction:** A premium editorial interface with an iridescent optical surface at the hero, dark ink and paper scenes below, retro-futurist status details, and restrained maximalist layering. The iridescence is a material treatment, not a rainbow decoration.

## Grammar and fingerprint gate

- Grammar: **Chaptered editorial**. Distinct scenes fit the requested retro-editorial structure and allow the hero to be maximalist while the lower page becomes calmer. The continuous-world grammar lost because Qaptr is a workflow concept rather than a physical place. Live surface lost because the page should persuade before it asks visitors to operate a product. Typographic poster lost because Qaptr needs product substance and a clear waitlist action. The remaining grammars lost because the page needs distinct cuts and a visible product story, not a single filmic device.
- Nav treatment: existing low-chrome sticky wordmark plus one CTA.
- Hero device: split hero with a material iridescent field and live process panel.
- Act sequence shape: material hero, process reveal, dial, editorial passages, Privacy Lens, held close.
- Close pattern: paper waitlist invitation.
- Signature move: Privacy Lens, where scroll changes the opacity and clarity of the same captured workspace while a green private boundary remains fixed in the composition.
- Fingerprint gate: clear against the empty registry. No prior rows exist.
